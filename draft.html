01 — About

Engineer by training, builder by habit.

I graduated from the University of Texas at Austin in 2025 with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (plus an Elements of Computing certificate) and went straight into defense tech. At Saronic I test autonomous surface vessels — the uncrewed ships redefining how navies operate — running field operations and hardware-in-the-loop testing on the Corsair program.

I started there as an intern and was promoted to R&D Test Engineer II in under two years. The through-line of my work is simple: own the thing that moves the needle — whether that’s a live-fire test on the water or a tool that saves the team a week.

Off the clock I ship software (usually AI-flavored), shoot pool, climb at Austin Bouldering Project, and follow macroeconomics and prediction markets more closely than is probably healthy.
